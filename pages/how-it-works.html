Three steps, one evening.

Add your guests

Type them, paste them, or upload your spreadsheet. Tag who's with who, and who really isn't. Mark couples, families, the rugby lads, and the two aunties who can't be within earshot of each other.

Build the room

Round tables, long tables, a top table, a dance floor. Drag guests onto seats, or press Sort it for me and let Vow Buddy solve the puzzle. It keeps couples together, keeps feuds apart, and tells you the moment something's wrong.

Print and share

A seating chart for the easel, place cards for the tables, table numbers that match, and a link your venue can open on the day. Change the plan at midnight and the venue link updates itself.

Why it's different

Every page already knows your date, your venue, your headcount and your guest list. Nothing gets typed twice. The caterer's dietary list writes itself from your plan, and both of you can edit from any device with one private link.
